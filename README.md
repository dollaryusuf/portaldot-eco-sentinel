## 🌐 Vision
**Portaldot Eco-Sentinel** is an autonomous monitoring and governance layer built to protect the sustainability of the Portaldot Network. While standard explorers track transactions, Eco-Sentinel audits the **environmental and structural integrity** of the digital civilization. 

By bridging raw Substrate telemetry with real-world **DePIN Energy Oracles**, we transform "Green" from a marketing term into a verifiable on-chain asset and a governance-enforced mandate.

---

## ✨ Key Technical Features

### 📊 Real-Time ESG Telemetry
A high-fidelity telemetry station that tracks **ESG Efficiency** and **State Fatigue** block-by-block. 
- **Fatigue Index:** A predictive engine that analyzes validation load to prevent infrastructure stress.
- **ESG Sync:** Real-time data streaming from DePIN oracles to monitor grid intensity (gCO2/kWh).

### 🕵️‍♂️ Deep Audit Engine
Utilizing the **Substrate-Interface**, Sentinel performs deep-packet inspection of extrinsic signatures. 
- **Verifiable Proofs:** Converts raw block weight into actionable carbon impact metrics.
- **Handshake Verification:** Audits historical block heights for anomalous energy consumption.

### ⚖️ Sentinel Democracy & Flags
A governance-integrated enforcement layer that ensures the network remains trusted.
- **Multisig Flagging:** Initiates on-chain flag calls for high-energy violations requiring 2/3 consensus.
- **LAO Integration:** Automatically bridges detection alerts into the Portaldot Governance (Democracy) pallet.

### 🌿 Node Certification (Soulbound Tokens)
Validators who maintain elite efficiency scores are awarded **Green Certificates**.
- **Tech:** ink! Smart Contract-based Soulbound Tokens (SBTs).
- **Function:** Acts as a permanent on-chain credential of a node’s contribution to the Green Civilization.
